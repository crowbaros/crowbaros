# Why our street needs a crossing

Every school morning, about forty children cross Mill Street at the corner by
the bakery. There is no crossing there. Cars come round the bend from the
bridge at thirty miles an hour, and drivers see the corner late.

A painted crossing with a raised table would slow them down. The council's own
survey from 2024 counted 1,100 cars an hour at that corner at 8 am. It costs
less than the resurfacing planned for next year, and could be done at the same
time.

I am asking the council to put the crossing on the agenda for the December
meeting.
